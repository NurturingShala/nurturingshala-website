An invitation to slow down

Reconnect with your body.
Make room for ease.

Our backs carry us through everyday life. This retreat is an invitation to pause and explore how we move, with curiosity, patience and care.

Whether you’re new to yoga or returning to your practice, we’ll explore the relationship between the feet, pelvis and spine through supported yoga, breath awareness and guided relaxation. Props and adaptations offer space to find what works for you.

Between practices, there is time to rest, share a meal or wander outdoors. You don’t need to fill every moment. You’ll explore ideas for a personal back-care practice to take home: support when your back feels tired, comfortable movement and greater awareness in sitting and standing.

During our time together

Practice, pause
and let it settle.

Supported backbends offer a glimpse of the approach we’ll explore: thoughtful alignment, helpful props and time to notice how you feel. Each practice is adapted to the person.

Find length and support

Ankit’s sessions explore how grounding through the feet and balancing the legs and pelvis can invite length along the spine and breadth across the back ribs. Twists develop gradually; supported backbends explore opening through the chest and upper back.

Explore with props

Drawing on Sharat Arora’s distinctive method, Ankit uses blocks, belts, chairs, bolsters and blankets to adapt the practice. These offer support and feedback, helping you notice unnecessary effort and find your own way into a posture.

Listen, soften and rest

Claire-Louise brings Scaravelli-inspired movement, Yin yoga and somatic awareness. Sustained, supported stillness, mindfulness and Yoga Nidra offer time to notice sensations, emotions and your sense of energy, with patience and curiosity.

Your teachers

Thoughtful guidance.
A warm welcome.

Ankit Kumar

Originally from India and now based in Brighton, Ankit is the teacher behind Nurturing Shala. He began practising yoga while volunteering at an ashram in the Himalayas after graduating. He holds a Master’s degree in Yoga, focused on traditional Hatha yoga and classical Indian philosophy, and a diploma in therapeutic massage. He has eight years of teaching experience.

Years of training under Sharat Arora at the Himalayan Iyengar Yoga Centre inform his attentive approach to alignment, breath and supported movement. His teaching adapts to modern bodies and busy lives while keeping awareness and connection with ourselves at its heart. He will lead our exploration of back and spinal wellbeing.

Claire-Louise Symonds

Claire-Louise has taught yoga since 2007. Her approach brings together Scaravelli-inspired and Yin yoga, mindfulness and nature connection.

Co-hosting and teaching alongside Ankit, she brings a gentle focus on somatic awareness, Yoga Nidra and rest.

Our home for the weekend

A place to practise
and pause.

In the foothills of the French Pyrenees, Viveka offers a dedicated yoga studio, welcoming shared spaces and gardens that lead into woodland.

Find a quiet corner on the terrace, spend time in the gardens and two woodlands, or settle into the sitting room with a book. There is also a meditation hut and a woodland sitting circle.

Planning your journey from the UK

A long weekend in France.
Space to arrive slowly.

The retreat runs from Friday 2 to Monday 5 April 2027 at Viveka Centre for Being, Le Ballé, Nescus, near Foix. Arrange your journey separately and contact Ankit to coordinate your arrival before booking travel.

By train

Can I come as a beginner or on my own?

All levels of yoga experience are welcome. You can book a private room or bell tent, or a place in shared accommodation. If you’re coming alone and considering a shared option, contact Ankit to discuss the arrangements.

What will the daily programme be?

The retreat brings together alignment-based yoga, supported postures, somatic and Yin practices, breath awareness, mindfulness and Yoga Nidra. Between sessions there is time for meals, rest and walks. Please contact Ankit for session timings and arrival/departure arrangements.

Meals & dietary requirements

Two vegetarian/vegan meals a day are included, cooked by Jon using locally sourced ingredients. No alcohol is served. Please discuss dietary requirements when booking.

Staying an extra night

An extra night costs €100, including a room, supper and breakfast, paid directly to the centre. Please confirm the date and availability with the centre before arranging travel.

Is this retreat right for me?

All levels of yoga experience are welcome. We’ll ask about injuries and health conditions when you book. Please contact Ankit to discuss your experience, access needs or whether the practice is suitable for you. This is a yoga retreat, not a medical treatment programme.

Deposit & remaining balance

The £200 deposit per person is non-refundable and forms part of your total retreat price. The remaining balance is due by 16 February 2027, 45 days before arrival. It is paid separately and will not be charged automatically.

What are the cancellation terms?

The £200 deposit is non-refundable. Please ask Ankit for the full cancellation and refund terms, including those for the remaining balance, before paying.

What should I bring?

Please contact Ankit for the packing and equipment details, especially if you choose a bell tent or need particular props or access arrangements.
