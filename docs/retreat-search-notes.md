# Retreat page: content and search decisions

Reviewed 7 October 2026.

## Source of retreat facts

The published `downloads/back-to-balance-retreat.pdf` is the primary source for the retreat programme, teacher descriptions, accommodation, meals, GBP prices and EUR extras. Existing authorised Stripe deposit checkout is retained. The brochure's ambiguous extra-night reference to Monday is presented without promising an extra-night date; guests should confirm directly.

## Page order and amount of information

1. Clear retreat identity, dates, France location, suitability and starting price; direct links to rooms and the brochure.
2. What visitors will explore and take home, using the brochure's specific account of alignment, props, somatic and Yin practice.
3. Actual Southampton practice photograph, attributed review excerpts and teacher biographies.
4. Venue and accommodation photographs, inclusions and time between sessions.
5. Full room/tent prices, both pricing periods, deposit, balances and secure checkout.
6. UK travel planning with official Eurostar and SNCF links, transfer costs and contact route.
7. Expandable secondary questions and a visible contact route.

This applies information-scent and progressive-disclosure guidance: use descriptive links, keep purchase-critical information visible, and allow people to expand secondary questions. There is no evidence-based magic word count for this retreat. Keep each passage useful, specific and readable; avoid keyword stuffing, urgency claims and guarantees about medical outcomes.

## Search implementation

- Descriptive page title and description, a canonical URL and British English language tag.
- Indexable static HTML, descriptive section headings and working internal navigation.
- Event JSON-LD with date-only dates (times are unconfirmed), physical venue, teachers, organiser and eight explicitly dated early-bird/standard offers. Prices represent the total retreat cost, not the deposit. No invented availability, aggregate ratings or retreat reviews.
- Open Graph/social preview metadata and relevant image alt text.
- Public robots.txt and XML sitemap; the main page links directly to the retreat.
- No special AI file or AI-only page, as Google does not require these.

## Remaining operational details

The owner has been asked to confirm arrival/departure times, teaching language and cancellation/refund terms beyond the non-refundable deposit. Packing, equipment arrangements and detailed session timings are not documented in the supplied brochure. The page directs visitors to contact Ankit rather than inventing them. Update these answers when confirmed.

Do not claim these submissions have occurred.

By 1 February 2027, update the visible leading price/description, room-card emphasis and expired early-bird offers. The checkout summary already changes to standard pricing automatically. Update availability when rooms fill; there is no live inventory on this static page. After the event, clearly mark it as past and replace booking links. Keep sitemap lastmod accurate when content changes.
